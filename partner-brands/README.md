# Partner brands

Third-party logos and marks included for reference when BBH appears alongside a partner. **These are not BBH assets.** Each file belongs to its respective owner and is governed by that owner's brand guidelines, not by this repository's license.

## Included

### BFH — Barcelona Finance Hub

`bfh/bfh-logo-variant-1.png`, `bfh-logo-variant-2.png`, `bfh-logo-variant-3.png`

The Barcelona Finance Hub is the current occupant of Passeig de Gràcia 19 — the former Barcelona Stock Exchange, and BBH's monthly venue. BBH is a guest. Whenever BFH is credited on BBH material (posters, event pages, social cards mentioning the venue), use one of these marks unmodified. Do not recolour, rotate, or inset anything into the BFH mark.

For questions about BFH brand usage, contact BFH directly. We do not speak on their behalf.

## Adding a partner

Before adding a new partner's logo to this folder:

1. Verify you have permission from the partner to include their mark in an open-source repository — most partners will be fine with this for "reference / attribution" purposes, but confirm.
2. Place files in a subfolder named with their short identifier (lowercase, kebab-case), e.g. `partner-brands/<name>/`.
3. Use the partner's official mark, unmodified. Prefer SVG; PNG acceptable as fallback.
4. Add a section to this README noting what the partner is, when BBH works with them, and the rough usage rule.

## Partner-less contexts

Most BBH material does **not** include partner marks. Use partner logos only when the partner is actually co-involved in the specific deliverable (co-hosting an event, sponsoring a talk, providing the venue). Gratuitous logo walls are off-brand.
